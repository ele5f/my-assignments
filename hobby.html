Interests

My Hobbies

📷 Photography 🎬 Video Editing
I have a passion for capturing moments through the lens — from food photography for brands to street and travel photography. Photography is both my hobby and a core part of my professional work. Editing videos is where I bring stories to life. I enjoy crafting reels, short films, and brand videos using various editing tools, experimenting with transitions, color grading, and sound design.
🎮 Gaming 🚶 Walking
Gaming is my way to unwind and challenge myself. I enjoy a variety of game genres and appreciate the creativity and storytelling behind game design, which also inspires my content creation work. I enjoy walking as a way to clear my mind and explore new places. Living in Bangkok gives me access to vibrant streets, parks, and neighborhoods that keep every walk interesting and refreshing.
🏊 Swimming 📱 Social Media Content
Swimming is my go-to physical activity. It keeps me active, focused, and energized. I find it both a great workout and a relaxing escape from daily routines. Beyond professional work, I personally enjoy creating and publishing content on social media — sharing my perspectives, daily life, and creative ideas with my audience across different platforms.

Reflection

How My Hobbies Shape My Work

Creativity Photography and video editing sharpen my eye for detail and visual storytelling, directly enhancing the quality of content I create for brands.
Discipline Swimming and walking build consistency and mental clarity, helping me stay productive and focused in both academic and professional environments.
Digital Fluency Gaming and social media keep me deeply connected to digital trends, platform behaviors, and audience engagement patterns.
